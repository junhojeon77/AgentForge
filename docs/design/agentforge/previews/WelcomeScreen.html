AgentForge

Welcome to AgentForge

Ask for something in plain words and a team of AI agents builds it on this computer. Nothing starts and no change is accepted without your OK.

How it works

  1. You ask for something in your own words.
  2. The lead agent turns it into a plan; you look it over and approve it.
  3. Your team builds it, each task in its own copy of your project.
  4. You review each change and approve or reject it.
How should the app look?

You can change this later at the bottom of the side list.

Want a short tour? It points at each part of the window in turn.
